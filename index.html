About Me

I am Qirui Liu (刘骐瑞), a master’s student at the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University (2026–Present), fortunate to be advised by Dr. Zhixuan Chu. Previously, I studied Computer Science and Technology at Northeastern University (2022–2026), advised by Changming Xu.

My research focuses on safety alignment of large reasoning models, efficient on-policy self-distillation, and reinforcement learning for language agents. I am interested in how models can learn from their own experience to reason, interact, and align more effectively.

I was a Research Intern at TruthLab (2025–2026), working on reasoning-model safety alignment, and previously a Research Intern at Wukong Lab, Northeastern University (2023–2025), working on deep reinforcement learning and computer games.

Find my work on Google Scholar and GitHub.

NeurIPS 2026 · Poster

SAVeR²: Reasoning-based Safety Alignment for Large Reasoning Models via Verifiable Rewards

Yichen Sun*, Qirui Liu*, Yan Wang, Jianan Lin, Linbo Jiang, Shiyu Wang, Zhibo Wang, Zhixuan Chu
